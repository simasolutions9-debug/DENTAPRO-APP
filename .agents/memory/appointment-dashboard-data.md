---
name: Appointment dashboard figures
description: Product rule for clinic dashboard appointment totals and revenue.
---

The dentist dashboard's daily income is a projection based on the tariff configured for each published clinic slot. It is not collected revenue. A slot without a configured tariff must remain explicitly unpriced rather than being treated as a zero-cost appointment.

**Why:** the user chose estimated income per appointment because the app has no payment records or payment integration yet.

**How to apply:** keep revenue labels clear that they are estimates; leave missing tariffs distinguishable from a free appointment; only report collected income after a real payment source is integrated.
